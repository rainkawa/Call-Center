📞 Call Center Tycoon - Game Guide

🎯 Your Goal

Build a successful outbound call center! Hire agents, buy leads, and make sales to grow your empire. Reach Day 30 with $10,000+ to win!

📊 Understanding the HUD

💵 Cash - Money for upgrades
👤 Agents - Your sales reps
📋 Leads - Warm prospects to call
⭐ Reputation - Higher = more answers
💰 Revenue - Today's earnings
🕐 Time - Work hours: 9AM-6PM

👤 How Agents Work

Orange = Working normally
Darker Orange = Tired (30% less productive)
Grey + Zzz = Sleeping (not working!)

💡 Get a Supervisor to auto-wake sleeping agents, or press F near them to wake manually.

📞 Leads vs Cold Calling

Warm Leads (purchased):
✓ 17% contact rate
✓ $70-150 per sale
✓ Less tiring for agents

Cold Calling (no leads):
✗ 5% contact rate
✗ $40-90 per sale
✗ 30% more tiring

🛒 Upgrades Overview

📋 Leads - More prospects to call
👤 Hire - More agents = more calls
👔 Supervisor - Auto-wakes agents (FREE!)
📝 Script - +5% conversion per level
📍 Local Presence - +8% answer rate
⚡ Power Dialer - 2x dial speed
☕ Coffee - Slower energy drain
🪑 Ergo Chairs - -20% energy drain
🤖 Predictive - +40% efficiency
🎧 QA Team - +10 reputation
📈 Analytics - Better optimization

🏆 Victory Milestones

🥉 Day 10 + $2,000
🥈 Day 20 + $5,000
🥇 Day 30 + $10,000
